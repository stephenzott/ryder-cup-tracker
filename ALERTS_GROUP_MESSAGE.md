⛳ Live score alerts for the Ryder Cup trip! Get a notification on your phone when holes, nines and matches are decided. Takes 2 minutes, please set it up this week:

https://the-cup-tracker.web.app/alerts.html

Open it in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. Not inside Instagram, Facebook or another app: if you tapped it there, open it in your browser instead.

📱 iPhone (iOS 16.4 or newer)
1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap the Share button, scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
3. Close Safari and open Cup Tracker from its new icon
4. Tap More → Alerts
5. Pick your name
6. Turn on Notify me about scores and tap Allow
7. Tap Send me a test notification: you should get one within a few seconds

🤖 Android
1. Open the link in Chrome
2. Recommended: tap the ⋮ menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen), then open Cup Tracker from its icon and go to More → Alerts
3. Pick your name
4. Turn on Notify me about scores and tap Allow
5. Tap Send me a test notification: you should get one within a few seconds

Choose what you hear about under What to send (every hole, segment results or just match finals) and Whose scores (your team, the other team or everything). Every hole gets busy on singles day!

Reply "got it" when the test notification arrives 👍
