Good to know

    Course handicap

    Course Handicap = Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Rating − Par)

    Index
    your Handicap Index
    Slope, Rating
    for the tee you play (see the tee charts below)
    113
    the slope of a course of standard difficulty
    Par
    the course's par
