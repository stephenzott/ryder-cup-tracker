Who are you?

Install the app (recommended)

Chrome can remove notification permission from websites people rarely open, but not from installed apps. Installing Cup Tracker keeps your alerts coming.

    Android tips

    Using Brave? If alerts don't arrive, open Brave's Settings → Privacy and security, look for a setting like “Use Google services for push messaging” and turn it on.

    Alerts arriving late?

    Android may hold back notifications to save battery. Open the phone's Settings → Apps, pick your browser (or Cup Tracker if you installed it), tap Battery and choose Unrestricted.
